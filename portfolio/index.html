About Me

Engineering digital products that work smoothly

I am a passionate Software Developer and undergraduate student pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) at the University of Sargodha.

My development experience centers on creating clean, responsive web applications, developing native Android solutions, and configuring customized e-commerce storefronts on Shopify. I approach every project with an engineering mindset: understanding client objectives early, writing well-structured and maintainable code, and ensuring the final user experience is fast, accessible, and dependable across all devices.

Career Interests: Seeking software engineering internships, junior developer positions, and freelance opportunities.
